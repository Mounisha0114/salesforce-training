## DAY-1 Notes 

### Salesforce Values
- Salesforce focuses on core values:
  - Trust
  - Customer Success
  - Innovation
  - Equality  
- These values guide how Salesforce builds products and serves customers  

---

### Salesforce Developer Role
- A Salesforce Developer builds applications on the Salesforce platform  
- Works with data, automation, and custom logic  
- Important because:
  - Businesses need customized solutions  
  - Developers help automate processes and improve efficiency  

---

### What is CRM?
- CRM (Customer Relationship Management) is a system used to manage customer data and interactions  
- Helps businesses track customers, sales, and communication  

---

### Why CRM is Needed
- Customer data is scattered across multiple systems  
- Difficult to track customer interactions  
- Lack of coordination between teams  
- CRM solves these by centralizing data and improving workflow  

---

### What is Salesforce?
- Salesforce is a cloud-based CRM platform  
- Used to manage customers, sales, and business processes in one place  

---

### Business Workflow
Lead → Contact → Opportunity → Customer  
- Lead → potential customer  
- Contact → confirmed person  
- Opportunity → sales deal  
- Customer → successful conversion  

---

### Sales Pipeline Concept
- A pipeline shows the stages of a sales process  
- Helps track progress of deals from start to finish  
- Improves visibility and decision-making  

---

### Salesforce Playground
- A Playground is a practice environment provided by Salesforce  
- Used to:
  - Learn Salesforce features  
  - Perform hands-on tasks  
  - Complete Trailhead challenges  

---

### Playground Management
- Create and manage multiple playgrounds  
- Used for testing without affecting real data  
- Helps in learning through practical experience

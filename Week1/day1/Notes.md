## DAY-1 Notes 

### What is CRM?
- CRM (Customer Relationship Management) is a system used to manage customer data and interactions  
- Helps businesses improve customer relationships and increase sales  

---

### What is Salesforce?
- Salesforce is a cloud-based CRM platform  
- Used to manage customers, sales, and business processes in one place  
- Provides a centralized system for all teams  

---

### Why Companies Use Salesforce
- Centralized customer data (single source of truth)  
- Better communication between teams  
- Automation of repetitive tasks  
- Real-time reports and insights  
- Improves efficiency and productivity  

---

### Core Salesforce Objects
- Lead → potential customer  
- Account → company/organization  
- Contact → individual person  
- Opportunity → sales deal  

---

### Business Workflow
Lead → Contact → Opportunity → Customer 

---

### Salesforce Playground
- A practice environment provided by Salesforce  
- Used to learn and perform tasks without affecting real data  
- Helps in completing Trailhead challenges

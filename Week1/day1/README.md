# Day 1: CRM Basics

## What is CRM?
CRM (Customer Relationship Management) is a system used to manage customer data, interactions, and business relationships. It helps companies improve customer satisfaction and increase sales.

## Why Companies Use Salesforce
- Centralized customer data (single platform)
- Better communication between teams
- Automation of tasks
- Real-time insights and reports
- Improved efficiency and productivity

## Core Concepts
Account
-Represents a company or organization.
Contact
-Represents a person associated with an account.
Opportunity
-Represents a potential sales deal.

## Business Workflow
Lead → Contact → Opportunity → Customer
- Lead: potential customer
- Contact: confirmed person
- Opportunity: sales deal in progress
- Customer: successful conversion

## Real-World Mapping (College Admission System)
- Account = College
- Contact = Student
- Lead = Student inquiry
- Opportunity = Admission process
